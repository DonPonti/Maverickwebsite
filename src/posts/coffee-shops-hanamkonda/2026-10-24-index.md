## What makes a coffee shop worth returning to?

A good coffee stop does not have to be expensive or elaborate. I would compare:

- **Taste:** Is the coffee balanced, or is it mostly sugar and milk?
- **Temperature:** Is hot coffee served hot and iced coffee properly chilled?
- **Consistency:** Would you expect the next cup to taste similar?
- **Price:** Is the drink worth the amount charged?
- **Comfort:** Is the seating suitable for how long you want to stay?

If you plan to work from a cafe, ask about Wi-Fi, charging points and laptop use. These features are not guaranteed just because a place has a modern interior.

## Hot coffee, filter coffee or cold coffee?

South Indian filter coffee has a different character from an espresso or latte. Cold coffee can range from lightly sweetened coffee to a dessert-style shake. There is no need to order the most expensive drink on the menu; start with the style you enjoy and compare the same drink at different places.
