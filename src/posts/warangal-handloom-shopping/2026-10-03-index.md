Warangal is not only about forts and temples. The region also has a long connection with handloom and textile work, and that side of the city deserves more attention.

If you are visiting Warangal and want to take something local home, textiles can be a much more meaningful choice than a generic souvenir.

## What should you look for?

Start by asking where the product was made, what material it uses and whether it is actually handloom. Don't assume that every saree sold in a local shop is automatically a handloom product.

If the seller provides a handloom mark or other relevant certification, ask to see it when appropriate.

## Why buy locally?

Apart from getting a product connected to the region, buying directly from local sellers and weavers can help keep traditional skills commercially useful.

The important thing is to buy because you like the product, not because somebody tells you that everything local is automatically better.

## Sarees are the obvious choice, but not the only one

Depending on the shop, you may find sarees, dress materials and other textile products. Availability changes, so it is better to visit a few places rather than deciding on one shop from an old article.

## A small shopping tip

Take your time. Check the fabric, finish, weave and price. If you are buying an expensive piece, ask questions instead of making a decision in five minutes.

### A better souvenir from Warangal

For me, the best local souvenir is something that has a story behind it. A handloom product from the region does that much better than a mass-produced item with a Warangal sticker.

If you are travelling specifically to shop, verify current store locations and opening hours before going because local businesses change.
